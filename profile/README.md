# Billington Lab

We are a multidisciplinary research group at Stanford University studying how the built environment affects people and how engineering and architectural design can better support human wellbeing.

## Our approach

Human interactions, responses, and contributions to built environments are often treated as an afterthought in engineering design. In the Billington Lab, people are central to our work.

We combine experimental methods with engineering, architecture, and social science to understand how designs and decisions affect human wellbeing—and to develop solutions that improve it.

## Our research

Our work examines the relationships among:

* **Buildings** — Designing and adapting spaces to support positive physical, psychological, social, and cognitive outcomes.
* **Nature** — Investigating human–nature relationships and the role of nature and biophilic design in wellbeing.
* **People** — Understanding how individuals and communities experience, respond to, and participate in decisions about the built environment.

Our research includes:

* Affordable housing
* Biophilic design
* Climate adaptation
* Ethical building-material supply chains
* Hybrid physical–digital spaces
* Human-centered infrastructure
* Sustainable materials

We use quantitative and qualitative methods to understand people’s experiences, develop research tools, and evaluate design interventions and adaptations.
